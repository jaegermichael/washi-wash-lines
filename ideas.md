## Chosen approach: Folding Mechanics

**Design movement:** Contemporary industrial product design, informed by product manuals, architectural elevations, and restrained premium homeware editorials.

**Core principles:**

1. The product is the evidence. Close-up material photography and the visible mechanics of opening and folding lead the story.
2. Space is an active demonstration. The site should feel as uncluttered and useful as the product’s promise.
3. Red only signals decisive action, moving parts, and key proof points. It is never a decorative wash.
4. Every section uses a different structural rhythm, avoiding a procession of generic cards.

**Color philosophy:** A graphite-black foundation makes the steel product and red operating cues feel engineered and durable. Off-white provides breathing room and lets real-home imagery feel clear rather than cold. Asphalt gray acts as the quiet technical layer behind specifications and product states. Washi red is the signature signal for conversion, movement, and measurable difference.

| Token | Value | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#050505` | Product-stage depth and authority |
| Washi Red | `#FF1616` | Action, moving parts, and conversion |
| Porcelain | `#F4F4F4` | Calm domestic space and legible contrast |
| Steel | `#151515` | Industrial surfaces and structural detail |
| Graphite Mist | `#8A8A8A` | Low-emphasis technical copy |

**Layout paradigm:** An architectural “unfolding” page. The hero establishes the full-width product theatre; the next sections progress from compressed side-by-side comparisons to a horizontally moving product rail, then a pinned three-step operation strip. Generous unoccupied space repeats the brand promise rather than relying on centrally stacked grids.

**Signature elements:**

1. A red articulated line that recurs as a seam, comparison handle, and product-mechanism indicator.
2. Expanded technical labels that use rules and tabular numerals instead of ornamental badges.
3. A folding-frame silhouette used in the logo mark and hero composition.

**Interaction philosophy:** Interactions should reveal product utility. The before/after control makes reclaimed space explicit, product selection changes clear, clearly marked example detail panels, and FAQ panels expose only real or clearly labelled pending information. CTAs always make it obvious that they open a WhatsApp enquiry, not a conventional checkout.

**Animation:** The initial product stage reveals slowly through opacity and small vertical movement. The folding-line motif shifts only in response to scroll or hover. The “Open. Dry. Fold.” sequence demonstrates the actual direction of motion with a small mechanism illustration, rather than decorative ambient loops. Motion turns off cleanly for reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography system:** Space Grotesk carries tightly tracked display headlines in bold weights. Manrope provides a compact, neutral body voice while IBM Plex Mono gives data labels, product placeholders, and mechanism annotations a purposeful technical character. Display headlines are short, left-aligned, and no more than two desktop lines.

**Brand essence:** Washi Wash Lines gives space-conscious homes a durable, folding alternative to permanent outdoor washing lines. **Modern, exacting, dependable.**

**Brand voice:** Confident and direct, using active verbs and useful, tangible language. Headlines are compact statements. CTAs name the action and destination. Example lines: “Dry more. Keep the view.” and “Tell us your space. We’ll help you choose.”

**Wordmark and logo:** A strong, all-caps wordmark paired with a solid red folding-frame mark that suggests a wall bracket opening outwards. The mark must work alone in the navigation and as the favicon; provided production logo assets should replace the generated stand-in without changing the layout.

**Signature brand color:** **Washi Signal Red, `#FF1616`.**

## Distinctiveness review

The concept avoids the generic “black page plus red accent” default by turning the foldable mechanism into a structural system. The red line has a clear job in each appearance, and imagery is planned as specific product documentation rather than generic home photography. The main risk, black and red feeling too aggressive for a domestic product, is controlled by shifting to porcelain-white sections that show uncluttered real-home contexts.

## Style Decisions

- The red folding-frame motif functions as a hinge, seam, measurement rule, comparison handle, or motion cue in every major section. It is never used as unconnected decoration.
- Product evidence prioritizes wall brackets, folded depth, line tension, wall mounting and real balcony or patio contexts, with domestic porcelain moments balancing the graphite stages.
- Copy stays practical and active, naming wall fit, folding, mounting, space, and WhatsApp help instead of generic supplier or lifestyle language.
